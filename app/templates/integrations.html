Connect a Messaging Channel

Your AI agent will respond to customers on this platform. Draft approvals can also be handled from here.

Setup Steps

  1. Go to Meta Business Suite
  2. Create a WhatsApp Business Account
  3. Go to WhatsApp > API Setup and generate a temporary access token
  4. Copy your Phone Number ID (this is your Account ID)
  5. Paste both below and click Connect

Note: Tokens expire after 24 hours in testing. For production, subscribe to the WhatsApp Business Platform for permanent tokens.

Setup Steps

  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather
  2. Send /newbot and follow the prompts
  3. BotFather will give you a bot token (looks like 123456:ABC-DEF1234ghIkl-zyx57W2v1u123ew11)
  4. Copy the token and paste it as the Access Token below
  5. For Account ID, enter your Telegram chat ID (your personal chat ID or a group ID)
  6. To find your chat ID: send any message to your bot, then visit https://api.telegram.org/bot<YOUR_TOKEN>/getUpdates

Optional — Production Webhook: For real-time message handling, set a webhook URL at https://api.telegram.org/bot<TOKEN>/setWebhook?url=https://yourdomain.com/api/v1/webhooks/telegram

Setup Steps

  1. Go to Meta for Developers
  2. Create a new App and add the Messenger product
  3. Generate a Page Access Token for your Facebook Page
  4. Copy the token and paste it as the Access Token below
  5. For Account ID, enter your Facebook Page ID
