Frequently asked questions

Is ServiceSync free for homeowners?

Yes. There's no cost to describe your problem, get matched, or chat with professionals. You only pay the professional's quoted price plus a small platform fee.

How does the AI matching work?

Our Gemini AI reads your natural-language description, extracts the trade type, severity, and location, then matches you with verified professionals based on their specialization, proximity, availability, and trust score.

What is escrow protection?

When you book a job, your payment is held in escrow (a secure third-party account). The professional only gets paid once you approve the completed work. If there's a dispute, our AI helps mediate.

Can I use WhatsApp or Telegram instead of the app?

Yes. Professionals can connect their WhatsApp, Telegram, or Messenger accounts. When a customer messages them, the AI handles the initial triage and can even draft replies for the professional to approve.

How do professionals get paid?

Once the customer approves the job, funds are released from escrow directly to the professional's account. Payouts typically process within 1-2 business days.
