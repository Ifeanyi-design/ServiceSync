Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ServiceSync collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and services. By using ServiceSync you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account data — name, email, phone, role (customer or contractor), and password (stored hashed).
  • Profile & job data — location, trade details, job descriptions, chat messages, and attachments you upload.
  • Payment data — processed by our payment provider (e.g. Stripe). We do not store full card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage data — basic logs needed to operate, secure, and improve the product.

How we use information

  • Match customers with professionals and power AI triage.
  • Enable messaging, escrow, receipts, and job status updates.
  • Send service notifications you enable in settings.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and technical issues.

Sharing

We share data with the other party in a job (e.g. your name and messages), payment processors, and infrastructure providers needed to run the service. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies

We use essential cookies (such as session authentication) so you can stay signed in and use core features. We do not use third-party advertising cookies in the MVP. You can control cookies in your browser settings; blocking essential cookies may prevent login.

Your choices

You can update profile and notification preferences in Settings, and request account deactivation by contacting support through the platform. For privacy questions, use the contact options on your account dashboard when available.
